About ekran

ekran aspires to be the quickest movie search: open the site, start typing, results appear as you type.

  • Live search with no page reloads, from any page
  • Focus the search box with / or ⌘K (Ctrl K)
  • Navigate results with ↑ ↓ or Ctrl N Ctrl P, open with Enter
  • Mark or unmark the movie you're viewing with m

Built by Matvey Smychkov. Movie data by TMDB.
